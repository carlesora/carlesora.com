CUPRA Gamified Car is a collaboration between the CITM–UPC and SEAT / CUPRA in which students work on gamified experiences for the car, using challenge-based learning. There were two editions, in 2022–23 and 2024.

The first edition was presented at the CIDUI 2023 conference on university teaching and innovation.
