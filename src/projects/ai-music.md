AI&MUSIC was a European S+T+ARTS project (Science, Technology and the Arts) on artificial intelligence and music, led by Sónar and the Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya together with Betevé.

The project paired UPC engineers with musicians to co-create new works using AI. One of them is *Piano + AI* with the pianist Marco Mezquida: an AI instrument that recognises the pianist's techniques and answers in real time with synthesised soundscapes, creating a feedback loop between musician and machine. It was presented at Sónar 2021 and 2022, L'Auditori de Barcelona and the Palau de Pedralbes, among other venues.

Carles Sora coordinated the research project and its co-creations, and programmed part of the festival held at the CCCB to close the process.
