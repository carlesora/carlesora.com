A research theatre project on the use of digital sonic technology on stage. *Quartet Lab* was an interactive sonic artwork inspired by the scenic space of the play *Quartet* by Heiner Müller. During the summer of 2012 we carried out an international creation residency at the Méduse centre in Quebec City, produced by Recto Verso.

The project had two fundamental goals: the search for new tools in theatrical language, and the desire to bring Quebec and Catalan culture closer together. In an increasingly interdisciplinary theatre context, the artists believed that new technologies should find their own voice on stage – collaborating transversally with theatrical tools, nourished by the reflection the text suggests: the incapacity of human beings to love, and language used not as a code to communicate but as a weapon to destroy.
