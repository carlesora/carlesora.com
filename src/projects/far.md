A collective performance for real-time musical and visual creation, inspired by the book *La pell freda* by Albert Sánchez Piñol, with drawings, paintings and software-generated images. It was presented at several electronic music and digital arts festivals, as well as artistic creation spaces, during 2006–07. The last presentation took place at the emblematic Sala Metrònom in Barcelona.
