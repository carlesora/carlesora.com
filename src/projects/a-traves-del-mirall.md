Joan Fontcuberta’s exhibition *A través del mirall* (Through the Mirror) is a multi-projection of self-portraits that transforms the space into a stage saturated with images, overflowing the subjective image exposed on social networks.

The exhibition includes an interactive device with a digital reflex camera and software that lets visitors add themselves to the exhibition through a portrait taken in front of a mirror. The photographs are shown on different projectors. The exhibition toured within the Catalan Department of Culture programme in 2012–13.
