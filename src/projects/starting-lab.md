UPF Starting Lab aims to provide undergraduate students with a professional learning and work platform to carry out highly innovative proposals in the digital audiovisual industry outside university. For six months of residency, selected projects follow an incubation method coordinated by Canòdrom – Creative Research Park, to develop final bachelor’s degree projects. Students have a work space where they receive professional advice and guidance.

Launched in December 2016, the incubator is an initiative of professor Carles Sora from the Audiovisual Communication programme at UPF, in collaboration with Canòdrom – Creative Research Park. It receives support from the Catalan Ministry of Culture via the Catalan Institute of Cultural Companies (ICEC).

The project cultivates the relationship between teaching, research and knowledge transfer, and promotes interdisciplinarity. It enables joint work by students from Audiovisual Communication, the Polytechnic School and the Faculty of Economic and Business Sciences, and fosters cooperation between alumni and undergraduate students.
