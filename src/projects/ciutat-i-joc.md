*Ciutat i Joc* (City and Play) explores games and play as ways for citizens to think about and reimagine the city. The project, run at the CITM–UPC, combined a card game and Minecraft game jams on urbanism and civic competences.

Results were published in the *Catalan Journal of Communication & Cultural Studies* (“Urbanism and Minecraft: designing a game jam to reimagine the city”) and presented at DiGRA 2023.
