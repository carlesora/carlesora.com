A procedural, experimental documentary. Since 2000, the artist Núria Manso has documented 300 interviews with artists and agents of the art world. This interactive project creates an automatic editing machine that mixes the interviews and makes them dialogue with each other, editing the video in real time from key concepts and subjects.

Users choose a few artists to start with, and the program creates a montage on the fly. Exhibited at the Arts Santa Mònica centre in Barcelona in 2011.
