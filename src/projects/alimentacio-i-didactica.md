Modular online interactives for student teachers, made of text, animations, interactive exercises and links, structured in units, modules and content nodes. There are two didactic units: the first has four modules and twenty-four nodes, the second three modules and twenty-three nodes.

Almost every node includes infographics, animations or interactive exercises, and each unit ends with an interactive concept map. Every node also includes a glossary, a reference bibliography and links of interest. A collaboration between Universitat Pompeu Fabra (Interactive Communication Experimentation group, IUA) and Universitat de Barcelona (Department of Didactics of Experimental Sciences and Mathematics), funded by AGAUR.
