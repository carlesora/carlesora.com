An interactive live video performance that uses cardboard vowels and a blank book to tell a tale, based on the book *Les Mystères de l’Alphabet*. The vowel characters are cartoons augmented within the live camera image, and the system lets the storyteller control the scenes.

It may be one of the first digital approaches to the craft of live interactive storytelling. It was presented at several digital arts events, including the VAD Festival of Digital Arts in Girona, the Poetry Festival of Córdoba and the International Performance Show in Mexico.
