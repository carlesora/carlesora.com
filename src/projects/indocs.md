INDOCS (*Interactive documentary and structured journalism: interactive storytelling, digital visibility, content curation, information visualisation and innovation*) studied interactive documentary and new forms of structured journalism.

One of its outputs is the report “Webdocs: social interaction and transmedia” (Freixa, Sora-Domenjó & Soler-Adillon, 2022).
