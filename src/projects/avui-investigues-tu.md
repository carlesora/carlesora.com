Interactive museography for the Museum of the Catalan Institute of Paleontology (ICP), designed and produced by the interdisciplinary studio Touché in 2009–2010.

The space had different interactive modules: a multitouch table for reconstructing dinosaur fossils, and an interactive journey where visitors experience being a scientist for a day. The journey is guided by a scientist character who accompanies visitors through different spaces on five multitouch screens. Visitors are identified with a card, so each one can follow the journey at their own pace. It includes 15 educational interactive games and 15 video clips that simulate a real scientific process of discovery.
