*Màgia per a Pixeloscopi* is an interactive experimental magic show (45′) in which the magician — and the audience — use augmented reality technology in the development of the magic tricks. It was a research project funded by the Phonos Foundation of UPF, in which interactive magic tricks were scripted and technically developed.

The show was presented in several venues and festivals, notably the Ingràvid art festival in Figueres and the CosmoCaixa auditorium in Barcelona during the Nit dels Museus 2010.
