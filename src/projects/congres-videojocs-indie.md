A congress on the independent video game sector, organised by the CITM–UPC in Terrassa with the support of the city council and the Barcelona Provincial Council.

Related work includes the report *El sector del videojoc indie a Catalunya* (2024) and the special issue of *Trípodos* on indie video games (2025).
