Regional S+T+ARTS Centers: Repairing the Present is a European Commission initiative within the S+T+ARTS programme (Science, Technology and the Arts). It brings together cultural centres, research institutions and companies across Europe to explore how art and technology can help address present-day social and environmental challenges.

The Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya took part as a third party through the CITM, with Carles Sora as principal investigator.
