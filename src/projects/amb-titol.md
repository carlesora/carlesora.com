*Amb títol* is an interactive documentary about how university can be a life-changing experience. It presents an interactive narrative through 30 testimonies of former students reflecting on their past experiences at university.

The webdoc fosters a participative dialogue on the role of public universities today. It was directed by the Catalan filmmaker Neus Ballús (Catalan original version with English and Spanish subtitles).
