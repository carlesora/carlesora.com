The 2017 Kosmopolis festival premiered the Stories Lab VR, a space for the exhibition of digital narratives, with activities around them such as round tables, talks and tasters. The theme chosen for the inauguration of the space was immersive narratives in 360° audiovisual formats and virtual reality.

The impact of this new wave of immersive narratives is ostensibly greater than previous ones, and possibly this time they are here to stay. This new format is generating a great deal of interest in spheres as diverse as journalism, art, documentary, museums and social activism.

At the height of the international boom in virtual reality, we wanted to generate a space for sitting back and reflecting on one of the most important debates present in all the international forums on this new format: its narrative capacities. The exhibition was divided into four spheres: documentary, independent narratives, children’s narratives and literary adaptations, and narratives for social change.
