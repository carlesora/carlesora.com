An interactive game designed to explain the gesture of framing in photography — and by extension in cinema — using paintings. The game proposes careful observation and the selection of a frame within the painting, and with it the creation of a story underlying the chosen image.

The exercise has three steps: observing the picture, choosing the format of the frame, and writing the story the image suggests. Once finished, the image is published and accessible to the rest of the school community. The interactive was part of the educational activities that the cultural association A bao a qu carried out in schools and high schools in Catalonia.
